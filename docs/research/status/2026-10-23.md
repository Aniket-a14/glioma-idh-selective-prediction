## Research phase

`PROJECT_STATE.md` says `current_phase: 06`, `status: in-progress`. Phase 06 stays open because the independent Gate D
review has not been done. Work since the last report:

- **Phase 04, preprocessing: complete (2026-09-30).** Visual quality control of 155 overlays excluded 6 UTSW patients
  (all `skullstrip-over`; the automated screen caught only 1 of the 6 at its first bound and was recalibrated, D-031).
  Intensity normalization statistics were fitted on the UTSW training split only and frozen, after a per-patient median
  rescale because 25 of 368 training patients had raw intensity scales 10 to 500 times the median (D-036).
- **Phase 05, split and pre-registration: complete and frozen (2026-09-30).** Patient-level split stratified on IDH,
  60/20/20, 368 / 124 / 124 patients from the 616-patient post-QC UTSW roster (D-034). The statistical analysis plan and
  the pre-registration are frozen with hashes; the C4 coverage band is +/-7 percentage points, with +/-5 secondary
  (D-004). The freeze commit predates any training commit.
- **Phase 06, model and training: nearly done.** A SegResNet IDH classifier (18.8 million parameters) with an auxiliary
  segmentation head was built. The seven-step authorization harness passed on the home GPU (RTX 2060 SUPER, 8 GB). Runs
  R1 and R2 trained to early stopping and their predictions are frozen. R2 on the internal test split (124 patients):
  AUROC 0.878 (95% CI 0.804 to 0.935), AUPRC 0.778, Brier 0.140, ECE 0.119. These are internal-split numbers from one
  training seed. Open: Gate D review, and D-017 (the GPU is smaller than the specification asks for; the A100 timeline
  is unresolved).
- **Phases 07 to 09, pulled forward on internal data only.** Calibration and conformal code (temperature scaling, split
  conformal, conformalized risk control) with independent cross-checks that agree; selective-prediction metrics checked
  by two implementations; head-only MC-dropout (D-048); the missing-modality condition sweep code; and the Phase 09
  statistics code (paired bootstrap, permutation test, Benjamini-Hochberg correction, rules for claims C1 to C6). On R2:
  split conformal at alpha = 0.10 covers 0.871 with 18.5% abstention; AURC is 0.0755 against an oracle of 0.0265. The
  Phase 09 pipeline has run on synthetic data only. Decisions D-048 to D-050 were resolved by the Research Lead on
  2026-10-09 and 2026-10-11.
